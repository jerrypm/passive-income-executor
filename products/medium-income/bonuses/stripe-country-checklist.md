# Stripe Express Country Checklist

A 1-page reference for Medium writers worldwide setting up Partner Program payouts via Stripe Express.

## Prerequisites Before Onboarding

- [ ] You are an active paying Medium member ($5/mo or $50/yr Member tier minimum)
- [ ] You are 18 years or older
- [ ] You have published at least 6 stories on Medium
- [ ] Your account is at least 3 months old
- [ ] You live in a country where Stripe Express is supported (see below)
- [ ] You have a bank account in your legal name in the country of residence

## Supported Regions Summary (100+ Countries Post-2024 Expansion)

| Region | Sample Countries | Notes |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| North America | US, Canada, Mexico | US writers complete W-9; non-US writers complete W-8BEN |
| Europe | UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, most EU | VAT not collected by Medium; declare locally |
| Asia | India, Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia, Vietnam, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Thailand, Singapore, Japan | Local-currency payouts in some markets |

| Latin America | Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru | Some countries support local currency |
| Oceania | Australia, New Zealand | Standard onboarding |

**Canonical, always-current list:** https://stripe.com/global

## Tax Documents By Region

- **US writers:** W-9 form. Income reported via 1099 if you cross $600 in a year.
- **Non-US writers:** W-8BEN form. Reduces US withholding from 30% to your country's treaty rate (often 0-15%).
- **EU writers:** No VAT collection by Medium. Declare as personal/freelance income locally. VAT registration may apply above local thresholds (~€10K-€50K).
- **All other regions:** Declare Medium earnings as personal income per your local tax rules. Keep a monthly earnings log for filing.

## Quick Wins For First-Time Setup

- [ ] Use a bank account in your legal name (joint accounts often fail)
- [ ] Have your government ID ready (passport or national ID)
- [ ] Set up Stripe Express in a single sitting — partial completions can lock for 24 hours
- [ ] If your bank rejects deposits: try a fintech wallet (Wise multi-currency, Revolut)
- [ ] Verify your tax form selection BEFORE submitting — corrections are slow

## If You Get Rejected

1. Wait 30 days before reapplying (cooldown is automatic)
2. During the wait: improve your weakest 2 stories, complete profile gaps, ensure paying-member status is uninterrupted
3. Reapplication uses the same form — submit fresh
