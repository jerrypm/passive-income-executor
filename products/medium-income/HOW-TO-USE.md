# How To Use This Guide

---

## The 14-Day Quickstart

The fastest path through this guide is Chapter 6: Your First Article in 14 Days. That chapter gives you a day-by-day action plan that runs from opening a blank document on Day 1 to submitting your finished story by Day 10 and applying to the Medium Partner Program by Day 12. You do not need to read the entire guide before you start — each chapter is short enough to read and act on the same day.

Alongside Chapter 6, use the printable bonus calendar (`bonuses/14-day-launch-calendar.html`) as your daily checklist. Open it in any browser and print to A4. Each day has a single primary task and a reading reference. If you follow the calendar, you will have published your first story and connected Stripe Express before two weeks are up.

---

## How To Use The Bonuses

- `bonuses/30-title-templates.md` — Fill-in-the-blank title formulas grouped by hook pattern; open in any text editor, pick a template that fits your niche, and fill in the blanks before you write a word of body copy.
- `bonuses/8-article-skeletons.md` — Full markdown article templates with `[FILL IN]` placeholders for every section; copy and paste directly into the Medium editor (or into a draft document) and replace the placeholders with your own content.
- `bonuses/14-day-launch-calendar.html` — Open in any browser and print to A4 for a wall-ready daily action checklist covering Days 1 through 14.
- `bonuses/stripe-country-checklist.pdf` — A one-page country reference for Stripe Express payout eligibility; print it or annotate it digitally while working through Chapter 9.
- `bonuses/medium-to-gumroad-funnel-template.md` — A step-by-step SOP for turning a successful Medium article into a Gumroad product, covering outline-to-PDF conversion, pricing, and linking your Medium story back to your Gumroad listing.
