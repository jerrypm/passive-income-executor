## TIER 1: DATA LABELING / AI TRAINING (Best ROI for Effort)

### 1. Remotasks (by Scale AI)
- **Apa**: Microtask platform untuk training AI — labeling, annotation, LiDAR, transcription
- **Real Earnings**: $1-2/hour untuk basic tasks, $5-15/hour untuk LiDAR/complex. Casual 10 hrs/week = $40-80/week ($160-320/month)
- **Indonesia**: YES, available. Payments via PayPal (min $5), Payoneer, Airtm, crypto
- **Payment**: Weekly (setiap Jumat)
- **Effort**: Medium — perlu training dulu, lalu task tersedia inconsistent
- **Automation**: NO — manual human judgment required
- **Verdict**: LEGIT, payment proof ada. Tapi task availability sangat tidak konsisten

### 2. Outlier.ai (by Scale AI)
- **Apa**: AI training platform — review AI outputs, write prompts, code review
- **Real Earnings**: $15-50/hour tergantung expertise. Average earners ~$2,500/year. BUT: Indeed rating 2.4/5 untuk pay
- **Indonesia**: Likely available (global), tapi task availability varies by location
- **Payment**: Weekly via PayPal (Tuesdays)
- **Effort**: HIGH — requires passing assessment, unpaid training period
- **Automation**: NO
- **Verdict**: HIGH PAY tapi inconsistent availability, rigorous unpaid onboarding. Worth trying kalau punya coding/STEM skills
- **Warning**: Banyak periode idle tanpa task

### 3. DataAnnotation.tech
- **Apa**: AI data annotation — review, label, annotate data for ML models
- **Real Earnings**: $15-30/hour, part-time $200-600/month, full-time $1,500-2,500/month
- **Indonesia**: Unclear — primarily US-focused but remote positions exist
- **Payment**: PayPal, withdraw setiap 3-7 hari
- **Effort**: Medium-High
- **Automation**: NO
- **Verdict**: LEGIT, good pay, tapi work flows in seasons — bisa berbulan-bulan tanpa task

### 4. Clickworker
- **Apa**: Microtask platform — surveys, data categorization, text creation, video recording
- **Real Earnings**: $5-9/hour average, some $10-15/hour. Tasks $0.02-$7.00 range
- **Indonesia**: YES confirmed. Payment via PayPal (Euro), min 10 Euro
- **Payment**: PayPal in Euro
- **Effort**: Low-Medium
- **Automation**: NO
- **Verdict**: LEGIT, confirmed working di Indonesia. Good as supplemental income

### 5. Toloka (ex-Yandex)
- **Apa**: Microtask / data labeling platform
- **Real Earnings**: $2-7/hour, most tasks < $4/hour. Tasks pay $0.01-$1.00 each
- **Indonesia**: YES (not in restricted countries list). Payment via Payoneer, PayPal (via Tipalti)
- **Payment**: Payoneer (available 200+ countries), min payout $1-5
- **Effort**: Low
- **Automation**: NO
- **Verdict**: LEGIT tapi very low pay. Good for idle time filler

### 6. Alignerr (by Labelbox)
- **Apa**: Premium AI training — cognitive labeling, decision evaluation, ethical AI
- **Real Earnings**: $22-39/hour, avg $60k/year full-time. VERY selective (lower acceptance than Harvard)
- **Indonesia**: Possibly, remote positions available globally
- **Payment**: Not specified in research
- **Effort**: HIGH — extremely selective onboarding
- **Automation**: NO
- **Verdict**: HIGHEST PAY in this category, tapi sangat sulit diterima

- **Verdict**: LEGIT DePIN project with real revenue. Season 2 airdrop distributing H1 2026. Staking $GRASS for fees now available
